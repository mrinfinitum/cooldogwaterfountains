=== Cool Dog Headless ===
Requires at least: 6.0
Requires PHP: 7.4
Stable tag: 1.0.0
License: GPL-2.0-or-later

Connects WordPress / WooCommerce to the Next.js website (www.cooldogwaterfountains.com).

* Sends visitors on WordPress pages to the same page on the website (optional switch).
  Cart, checkout, My Account, admin, previews and logged-in editors stay on WordPress.
* Refreshes the website within seconds when pages, products, prices, stock or categories change.
* Points WooCommerce "Return to shop" and product links at the website.

Settings → Cool Dog Headless: website address, refresh secret (same as REVALIDATE_SECRET
in Vercel), redirect switch, and a "Test refresh now" button.
